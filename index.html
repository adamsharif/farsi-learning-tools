The Persian alphabet

Persian is written from right to left. Focus a word or hover its letters to see Pinglish. Context forms below are shaped by real neighboring letters—not by stretching marks.

How letters connect

Most Persian letters connect to letters on both sides. These seven letters—ا د ذ ر ز ژ و—connect only to the preceding letter, never to the next one.

Joining behaviorLettersWhat happens
Connect both sidesب پ ت ث ج چ ح خ س ش ص ض ط ظ ع غ ف ق ک گ ل م ن ه یJoin to the preceding and following letters in a word.
Connect only to the preceding letterا د ذ ر ز ژ وThey join from the right, but break the connection to what follows.
Non-connectingNone in the standard Persian alphabetEvery letter can connect to a preceding letter; seven cannot continue the join.
